# Tax rules per instrument type for an individual, and their official sources

Wayfinder research ticket #611, part of map #510 (portfolio-diagnosis demo).
Position date: **2026-09-30**. Researched 2026-10-05 about 09:20 to 09:50 UTC-3
(12:20 to 12:50 UTC). Every rule below comes from the text of the law or decree on
`planalto.gov.br`, or from the Receita Federal's own publication on
`gov.br/receitafederal`; each is quoted with its URL in section 3. No database was
read and nothing was written anywhere. No real portfolio data is used. This is a
research note: the YAML files in section 5 are a draft and are not added to `src/`.
An addendum at the end (2026-10-05, about 10:00 to 10:15 UTC-3) closes five items of
section 6 from primary sources and corrects one row of section 4.

Scope: an individual resident in Brazil (pessoa física residente), income tax (IR)
and IOF only. Not covered: IBS/CBS, ITCMD, non-residents, companies.

## Answer

1. **On 2026-09-30 the investment rules are the pre-2025 rules, plus three 2026
   changes at the level of the person, not of the instrument.** No law, MP or
   decree in force on that date changed the IR rate or exemption of any instrument
   in the ticket. The one that tried, **MP 1.303/2025** (a flat 17.5% rate, 5% on
   LCI/LCA/CRI/CRA/incentivized debentures, effects "a partir de 1º de janeiro de
   2026"), **lapsed on 2025-10-08** (Ato Declaratório do Congresso nº 67/2025, DOU
   2025-10-15) and was never converted. None of its rates ever applied. Planalto
   strikes its references through in every law it touched and marks them
   "Vigência encerrada".
2. **What did change for 2026:**
   - **Dividends:** above R$ 50,000 in a month from one company to one person,
     10% withheld at source from January 2026 (Lei 9.250 art. 6º-A, by Lei
     15.270/2025), except profits up to 2025 approved by 2025-12-31.
   - **Minimum tax on high incomes** (above R$ 600,000 a year) from calendar
     year 2026 (Lei 9.250 art. 16-A). Its base explicitly **excludes** the income
     of LCI, CRI, LCA, CRA, Lei 12.431 assets and FII/Fiagro distributions (with
     100 holders), so those exemptions survive it. Dividends are in the base.
   - **JCP** (interest on equity) withholding goes from 15% to **17.5%** (Lei
     9.249 art. 9º §2º, by LC 224/2025 art. 8º, effects 2026-01-01).
   - **IR monthly table and reduction:** a new monthly table from May 2025
     (Lei 11.482 art. 1º XII, by Lei 15.191/2025) and a reduction for incomes up
     to R$ 7,350 a month from January 2026 (Lei 9.250 art. 3º-A). These touch only
     the progressive PGBL/VGBL regime.
   - **IOF on VGBL contributions:** 5% on the part of a person's yearly VGBL
     contributions above R$ 600,000, from 2026-01-01 (Decreto 6.306 art. 22 §1º
     V, by Decreto 12.499/2025). Planalto shows this as the current text, beside
     a Decreto Legislativo 176/2025 that sustained it and a "Vide ADC nº 96"
     note. The addendum reads the STF case: a relator's injunction of 2025-07-16,
     still awaiting the Plenary, restored Decreto 12.499 from its issue, and the
     VGBL clause is not among the parts it kept suspended.
3. **LC 224/2025 (the 10% cut of federal tax benefits) does not reach these
   exemptions.** Its art. 4º §1º lists the taxes whose benefits are cut:
   PIS/Cofins, IRPJ/CSLL, II, IPI and the employer's social security
   contribution. The individual's income tax (IRPF) is not on the list.
4. **The rules per instrument**, as of 2026-09-30 (full table in section 2):
   - **Fund with come-cotas, long term:** 15% every last business day of May and
     November, topped up at redemption to the 22.5 / 20 / 17.5 / 15% table by
     holding period (Lei 14.754 art. 17).
   - **Fund with come-cotas, short term** (portfolio average term ≤ 365 days):
     20% periodic, topped up to 22.5% up to 6 months, otherwise 20% (Lei 14.754
     art. 17 §1º II, Lei 11.053 art. 6º).
   - **Equity fund (FIA, ≥ 67% in shares):** no come-cotas; 15% at redemption
     (Lei 14.754 art. 18 parágrafo único, 21, 24).
   - **CDB, common debenture:** 22.5 / 20 / 17.5 / 15% by days held (≤ 180,
     181–360, 361–720, > 720), withheld at source (Lei 11.033 art. 1º).
   - **LCI, LCA, CRI, CRA:** exempt for the individual, at source and in the
     annual return (Lei 11.033 art. 3º II and IV).
   - **Incentivized debenture (Lei 12.431 art. 2º):** 0% for the individual,
     only for assets issued up to 2030-12-31 (art. 2º §1º).
   - **FII:** distributions exempt only if the quotas trade exclusively on an
     exchange or organized OTC market, the fund has ≥ 100 holders, the person
     holds < 10% and linked persons < 30% (Lei 11.033 art. 3º III and §1º);
     otherwise 20%. Capital gain on sale: 20% (Lei 8.668 art. 18), no R$ 20,000
     exemption.
   - **Shares:** 15% on monthly net gain (20% day trade); exempt when the month's
     **sales** of shares are ≤ R$ 20,000 (Lei 11.033 art. 2º and 3º I).
   - **Equity ETF:** 15% on sale (20% day trade), **no** R$ 20,000 exemption
     (Receita, P&R IRPF 2026 Q707); 15% at redemption or distribution (Lei 14.754
     art. 24) when it is an "entidade de investimento", else 15% with come-cotas
     (art. 26).
   - **Fixed income ETF:** 25 / 20 / 15% by the portfolio's average repricing
     term (≤ 180, 181–720, > 720 days), 30% if it breaks the 75% rule, withheld at
     sale, redemption or distribution (Lei 13.043 art. 2º).
   - **PGBL and VGBL, regressive regime:** 35 / 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 / 10% by
     accumulation time (≤ 2, 2–4, 4–6, 6–8, 8–10, > 10 years), definitive (Lei
     11.053 art. 1º). **Progressive regime:** 15% withheld as an advance, on the
     whole redemption for PGBL and on the income for VGBL, then the annual
     progressive table (Lei 11.053 art. 3º). The choice can be made up to the
     first benefit or redemption and is irrevocable (art. 1º §6º, by Lei
     14.803/2024).
5. **IOF** (Decreto 6.306): a redemption within 30 days pays IOF on a falling
   share of the income (96% on day 1 to 0% on day 30, art. 32 and Anexo) for CDB,
   LCI and funds. Zero rate for equity funds, the variable-income market (shares,
   FII and equity ETF on the exchange), debentures, CRI, CRA, LCA and
   fixed-income ETF trading (art. 32 §2º). A fund redeemed before its grace period
   pays 0.5% a day (art. 31).
6. **What the engine cannot see from a statement**, and so must flag rather than
   guess: whether a debenture or CRI is under Lei 12.431 art. 2º and its issue
   date; whether an FII meets the 100-holder, 10% and 30% tests; whether an ETF
   or FIDC is an "entidade de investimento"; a fund's short or long term class;
   the PGBL/VGBL regime chosen and the date of each contribution; and the person's
   total income (minimum tax) and other dividends from the same company.
7. **Recommendation:** one YAML per instrument type as the owner designed
   (section 5), each rule carrying its article, URL, quote and `valid_from`, with
   the sha256 recorded like `indexer_rules.csv`. Model the 2026 person-level
   changes (dividends, minimum tax, JCP) in a separate `person.yaml`, not inside
   the instrument files, and put the conditions the engine cannot observe in an
   explicit `engine_cannot_observe` list that the report prints as "a conferir".

## 1. Method

**Sources.** Only `planalto.gov.br` (laws, decrees, MP, the Congress' acts, the
legislation index pages) and `gov.br/receitafederal` (the "Perguntas e Respostas
IRPF 2026", version 1.00 of 2026-04-23). `normas.receita.fazenda.gov.br`, where
IN RFB 1.585/2015 lives, answered "Acesso negado" to the scraper, so the IN is
cited only through the Receita's own P&R, which quotes its articles. Direct HTTPS
to planalto and normas from this container is refused by the egress proxy; pages
were read through Firecrawl (until its credits ran out) and then Exa.

**Reading planalto's compiled texts.** A compiled page keeps every past wording,
struck through, beside the current one. The rule applied: the current wording is
the text not struck through with the latest "Redação dada pela". Where the
scraped markdown was ambiguous (the FII 100-holder clause of Lei 11.033 art. 3º
§1º I shows a Lei 14.754 wording, an MP 1.184 wording marked "Vigência encerrada",
and the Lei 14.754 wording again), the wording kept is the one whose amending act
is still in force (Lei 14.754), since the MP lapsed.

**Search for 2025 and 2026 changes.**

- The "Vide" and "Redação dada pela" notes in each compiled text read (Lei 11.033,
  11.053, 12.431, 13.043, 14.754, 8.668, 11.482, Decreto 6.306). The only 2025-26
  acts they cite on these articles are MP 1.303/2025 (lapsed), LC 214/2025 (its
  art. 511 and 527 change the REPORTO and IPI articles of Lei 11.033 and 12.431,
  not the investment ones), Lei 15.191/2025 (IR table) and Decretos 12.466,
  12.467 and 12.499/2025 (IOF).
- Planalto's index of MPs 2023 to 2026, read to MP 1.394 of 2026-09-25: no MP
  after 1.303 deals with the taxation of investments.
- Planalto's index of complementary laws, read to LC 237 of 2026-09-15: LC 224
  (2025-12-26), 229 (2026-03-30) and 237 (2026-09-15) were read in full; 229 and
  237 only exempt some future bills from the LRF limits.
- Planalto's index of 2026 ordinary laws, through Exa's copy, which ends at Lei
  15.491 of 2026-08-24: no ementa touches investment income tax. Laws 15.492
  onward (the MP index cites up to Lei 15.503) were **not** seen in that list.
- Web search (Exa, WebSearch) for "2026 LCI LCA imposto", "MP 2026 aplicações
  financeiras" and similar returned only MP 1.303 coverage.

This is a scan of the acts that amend the instruments' own laws, not an
exhaustive scan of everything Congress passed in 2025 and 2026.

**Verification of the quotes.** The quotes in section 3 from Lei 11.033, 12.431,
13.043, 14.754, MP 1.303, Decreto 6.306 and the P&R were checked by a script
against the saved page text (whitespace and markdown removed): all match. The
quotes from Lei 11.053 and the ADC 67 were matched again on a second, live fetch
through Exa. The quotes from Lei 8.668, 11.482, 15.270, LC 224 and DL 176 were
copied from the fetched page text and checked by reading, not by script (Firecrawl
ran out of credits before they were saved). The pages were not all live reads:
the "Accessed" column says, for each one, whether Firecrawl served a live fetch or
a cache entry and of what date.

## 5. Draft YAML schema for `src/portfolio/rules/tax/<instrument>.yaml`

Owner's design (2026-10-05): one file per instrument type, each with a version,
the article, the URL and the date in force; the engine records each file's sha256
as it does for `src/portfolio/rules/indexer_rules.csv` (`indexer.rules_sha256`,
reported as `rules_sha256` and `rules_file`). Proposed file names: `fund_long.yaml`,
`fund_short.yaml`, `fund_equity.yaml`, `cdb.yaml`, `lci.yaml`, `lca.yaml`,
`cri.yaml`, `cra.yaml`, `debenture_incentivized.yaml`, `debenture.yaml`,
`fii.yaml`, `shares.yaml`, `etf_equity.yaml`, `etf_fixed_income.yaml`,
`pension_regressive.yaml`, `pension_progressive.yaml` (with `plan: pgbl | vgbl`
inside), plus `iof.yaml` (the shared 30-day table) and `person.yaml` (dividends,
JCP, minimum tax, the progressive table), which are not instruments but are
referenced by them.

Schema (every key required unless marked optional):

```yaml
schema: silo.tax_rule/0.1 # schema version of this file format
instrument: cdb # file stem; one instrument type per file
version: 1 # bumped on any change; the engine also records sha256
valid_from: 2005-01-01 # first day this file's rules apply
valid_to: null # last day, or null while in force
position_date_checked: 2026-09-30 # date the sources were last read against
sources: # every rule below cites one of these by id
  - id: l11033_art1
    act: Lei 11.033/2004
    article: art. 1º, I a IV
    url: https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2004-2006/2004/lei/l11033.htm
    accessed: 2026-10-05
    quote: "IV - 15% (quinze por cento), em aplicações com prazo acima de 720 (setecentos e vinte) dias."
ir:
  events: # when IR is due
    - event: redemption # redemption | maturity | coupon | sale | distribution | periodic
      withheld_at_source: true
      definitive: true # false = advance against the annual return
      base: income # income | gross_redemption | net_gain_month
      rate:
        table: holding_days # fixed | holding_days | accumulation_years | repricing_term_days | progressive
        rows: # [min, max] inclusive; max null = no upper bound
          - { min: 0, max: 180, rate: 0.225 }
          - { min: 181, max: 360, rate: 0.20 }
          - { min: 361, max: 720, rate: 0.175 }
          - { min: 721, max: null, rate: 0.15 }
      source: l11033_art1
  periodic: null # come-cotas: {dates: [last_business_day_may, last_business_day_nov], rate: 0.15, complement_to: <table>}
  exemptions: [] # e.g. {condition: monthly_sales_le, value: 20000, source: ...}
iof:
  applies: true
  rule: iof_30_day_table # a key of iof.yaml; or zero_rate
  source: d6306_art32
engine_cannot_observe: [] # conditions the statement does not show; the report prints them as "a conferir"
not_verified: [] # anything from section 6 that touches this instrument
```

Example of the conditions list, for `fii.yaml`:


### 4. Lei 14.801/2024 (debêntures de infraestrutura): the rule for an individual

Not exempt. The individual pays the regular fixed-income table at source,
definitively (art. 2º caput, art. 3º caput and II), and the Lei 12.431 zero rate
"não se aplica" (art. 3º §2º). Only debentures issued from 2024-01-10 to
2030-12-31 meeting Lei 12.431 art. 1º §§1º, 1º-C and 2º qualify (art. 2º §5º). For
the issuer the benefit is a 30% extra deduction of the interest (art. 6º II), and
exempt funds pay 10% (art. 4º). Lei 15.506/2026 changed only art. 2º §2º II and
added §10 (critical minerals); planalto marks the MP 1.303 references "Vigência
encerrada".

So for the engine a Lei 14.801 debenture is a **common debenture** (22.5 / 20 /
17.5 / 15%), not an incentivized one. The two cannot be told apart from the name
"debênture incentivada" on a statement: the issue's deed or prospectus says which
law it was issued under. Add it to `engine_cannot_observe` for `debenture.yaml` and
`debenture_incentivized.yaml`. No new row in section 2.

### 5. Solução de Consulta Cosit 28/2026: VGBL paid on death

It exists: **Solução de Consulta Cosit nº 28, of 2026-02-25, published in the DOU
of 2026-03-04** (section 6 had 2026-02-25 as the publication date, from the press;
that is the date of the act). Signed by the Coordenador-Geral de Tributação; it
binds the Receita. For the beneficiary of a VGBL on the holder's death:

- the **risk cover** (capital for death) is exempt;
- the balance of the **PMBaC** (accumulation phase) is taxed at 15% at source as an
  advance, on the income (amount received less premiums paid), or definitively by
  the regressive table if that regime was chosen;
- the balance of the **PMBC** (payout phase) is taxed by the monthly progressive
  table, on the same base, or definitively by the regressive table.

This is outside a living holder's portfolio and changes no row; it belongs in a
note on the VGBL rows ("on death, see Cosit 28/2026").
