| 2026-10-05 | research/quota-net-of-fees | **Research #610: the Informe Diário quota is net of the fund's fees.** `docs/reference/research/quota-net-of-fees.md` quotes the primary texts: the quota is PL ÷ quotas (Res. CVM 175 Parte Geral Art. 14 § 1; ICVM 555 Art. 11 § 1); administração, gestão and distribuição are encargos debited from the class (Art. 117 XVI, XVIII), and performance and custody are FIF encargos (Anexo I Art. 77); in open classes the service providers' fees "devem ser provisionadas por dia útil, sempre como despesa da classe" (Art. 117 § 2; ICVM 555 Art. 85 § 3 names performance too). The performance fee is provisioned, not only booked when paid (Anexo I Art. 28 §§ 5-6, Art. 22 § 1), and is computed after all other expenses (Art. 28 § 1 IV). ETFs may not charge one (Anexo V Art. 7). CVM's `meta_inf_diario_fi.txt` says only "Valor da cota". Not verified: the COFI, the método-do-ajuste quota, closed classes, the non-fee encargos' timing.
