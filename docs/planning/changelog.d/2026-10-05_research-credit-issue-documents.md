CVM RAD (debenture escrituras) and B3 Fundos.NET (termos de securitização) serve text-layer PDFs with no login or captcha; three public examples (one debenture, one CRA, one CRI) read by hand for issuer CNPJ, lastro, debtor, guarantees, indexer, maturity and rating. SILO's `cia_event` already indexes 1,108 RAD escrituras and aditamentos (2025-01 to 2026-10, 297 issuers); CRA and CRI documents sit on Fundos.NET's certificados page (`paginaCertificados=true`, tipoFundo 5 and 6), which the FNET fetcher does not query. ANBIMA Data rejected for automated reading (terms and a JavaScript-only page). Research only; no code, schema or data change. |
