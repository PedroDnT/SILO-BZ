| 2026-10-14 | orchestrator/955-rj-retry-503 | **The RJ collector retries an HTTP 503 once (#955).** On 2026-10-13 one transient 503 on an `inova-aj.com.br` case page turned the slice red. `PoliteClient._get` now retries a GET answered with 503 once. The retry waits the per-host interval, or the answer's `Retry-After` in seconds when it is longer, capped at `RETRY_AFTER_CAP_S` (30 s). An HTTP-date or unreadable `Retry-After` uses the normal interval. A dropped connection (#943) and a 503 share the one retry, so a GET makes at most two attempts. No other status is retried, and a second 503 still raises `FetchError` with status 503. Offline tests in `tests/test_rj_collector.py` cover each case on an injected clock and sleep. |
