| 2026-10-05 | research/pension-plan-data | **Research note for #612 (map #510): what public data shows about a PGBL or VGBL line, `docs/reference/research/pension-plan-data.md`.** SUSEP's open data (Olinda `produtos`, Consulta Pública, SES) identifies the plan by processo, insurer and ramo. It has no loading fee and no FIE: the SES fund tables hold one provision total per insurer and month. The only machine-readable public link from a processo to its FIE CNPJ is the insurer's Open Insurance phase-1 product data (`/products-services/v3/life-pension`: `susepProcessNumber`, `investmentFunds[].cnpjNumber`, the carregamento as a min-max range); the note read the specification only, not a live endpoint. The tax regime is a phase-2 contract field behind the client's consent and is public nowhere. In SILO the FIE is an ordinary fund: 5,090 latest Extrato rows carry a `PREVIDÊNCIA` ANBIMA class, 2,927 of the 3,645 that reported in 2026-09-24..30 have one quotaholder, and no CVM column flags an FIE. Public example: XP Seg Superprev FIM (35.420.569/0001-90), with its PGBL and VGBL processos, an Extrato fee of 2.6 against the sheet's 0,60% effective rate (both as filed), and 29 funds held in CDA block 2 (2026-08). Recommendation: keep the CNPJ the statement prints as the FIE key, take the carregamento only from the client, and show both tax scenarios. Research only; no code or schema change. |
