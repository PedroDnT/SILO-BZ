## Supported Data Sources

### CVM (Comissão de Valores Mobiliários)
Access credit market data including:
- **FIDC**: Receivables Investment Funds
- **FIP**: Private Equity Investment Funds
- **FIAGRO**: Agribusiness Investment Funds
- **SECURIT**: Securitization operations (CRA, CRI, LCA, LCI)

### BACEN (Banco Central do Brasil)
Access over 18,000 economic time series:
- **SGS**: Time Series Management System
- **PTAX**: Official exchange rates
- **Expectativas**: Market expectations (Focus)
- **Taxas de Juros**: Interest rate data

### B3 CALC
Fixed income pricing and market data:
- **Debentures**: Corporate bonds
- **CRA**: Agribusiness receivables certificates
- **CRI**: Real estate receivables certificates
