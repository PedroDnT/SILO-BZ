### Overview

| Function | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| `portfolio_fee_peers(p_cnpjs, p_as_of)` | A fund's administration fee against the active FI funds of its exact ANBIMA class. |
| `class_return_distribution(p_classe_anbima, p_fundo_cotas, p_month)` | What a class's 12 and 6 month returns look like across its funds. |
| `portfolio_equivalents(p_classes, p_as_of)` | The active ETFs that track an index mapped to an ANBIMA class. |
| `portfolio_credit_returns(p_codes, p_end_month, p_series, p_classes)` | A CRA or CRI's value on the securitizer's curve. |
| `portfolio_debenture_returns(p_tickers, p_end_month, p_max_monthly_drop_pct)` | A debenture's mark as the funds that hold it report it. |

### `portfolio_fee_peers`

Compares the fee with the active FI funds of the same ANBIMA class, `FUNDO_COTAS` flag and document scope. Active ETFs join a class only when their `underlying_index` is mapped to it in an owner-reviewed list. An ETF's fee comes from a third-party site (etfsbrasil), never from CVM, and `etf_peer_fee_source` says so. Fewer than 30 usable fees gives no statistic, and the function does not widen the group.

### `class_return_distribution`

Two rows, with `window_months` 12 and 6, ending at the close of `p_month` (NULL means the last complete FI month). It gives `p25_pct`, `median_pct` and `p75_pct` over `n_funds` returns, and counts the funds it left out. With fewer than 30 funds or an incomplete month, the row is `nao_avaliado`, with NULL statistics and a reason. ETFs are not in the universe.

### `portfolio_equivalents`

Takes 1 to 50 classes as filed. `is_equivalent` marks the ETF with the largest PL (`pl_rank` 1) across the class's indices. A class with no approved index pair (`sem_par`), no active ETF (`sem_etf`) or no PL (`sem_pl`) returns NULL ETF columns. PL and fee are third-party values with their dates. It names an ETF with the same objective, not a recommendation.

### `portfolio_credit_returns`

Takes 1 to 70 CETIP codes (a leading `CRA-` or `CRI-` is stripped) and returns up to 13 month-ends from the securitizer's filings. `pu` is `valor_certificados / quantidade_certificados`, and `factor` is (pu + paid) / previous pu. A `month_flag` makes a month unknown, for example `mes_ausente`, `pu_repetido` or `queda_sem_evento_arquivado`. A fall in PU with no filed payment is never read as a loss. It is the securitizer's value on its curve, not a market price.

### `portfolio_debenture_returns`

Takes up to 70 debenture tickers and returns 13 month-ends from CVM CDA block 4. For each month it takes the median PU across funds (at least 3 funds), and `factor` is that median over the previous month's. A fall beyond `p_max_monthly_drop_pct` is flagged `queda_pu_possivel_evento`; with a NULL threshold every fall is flagged `limite_pendente`. It is the funds' marks, not a trade price, and it invents no coupon or amortization.
