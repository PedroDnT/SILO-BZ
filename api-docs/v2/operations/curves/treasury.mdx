## Source and licence

This data is Tesouro Nacional open data ("Taxas dos Títulos Ofertados pelo Tesouro Direto"), under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Every row carries `source` (`Tesouro Nacional`) and `licence` (`ODbL-1.0`). When you reuse it, credit Tesouro Nacional, and offer a database you derive from it under the same licence.

The rates are the platform's posted morning buy and sell quotes, in % a year as published, not trades. They can be negative. A null price is a side the title was not on sale on. With no dates, the newest base date comes first; one base date holds about 60 titles.
