### Overview

Two functions over BACEN data Silo already ingests daily. Nothing is derived —
no annualisation, compounding, averaging, mid rate or fill. IPCA is not here: it
is [`inflation`](/api-docs/inflation), with its own units and caveats.

- **`macro_series(p_series, p_from, p_to)`** — one SGS series, oldest first.
- **`ptax(p_currency, p_from, p_to)`** — PTAX buy and sell for one currency.

```python
silo.macro_series("CDI")                 # last 12 months
silo.macro_series("SELIC_META", start="2020-01-01")
silo.ptax("USD")
```

### The series

`p_series` takes the label or the SGS code. Read `unit` on every row before doing
arithmetic.

| Label | SGS | `unit` | `frequency` | Is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SELIC_META` | 432 | `pct_year` | daily | the Copom target, % a.a. |
| `SELIC_DIARIA` | 11 | `pct_day` | daily | the Selic rate, % per business day |
| `CDI` | 12 | `pct_day` | daily | the CDI, % per business day |
| `IGPM` | 189 | `pct_month` | monthly | IGP-M, % change in the month |
| `INPC` | 188 | `pct_month` | monthly | INPC, % change in the month |
| `POUPANCA` | 25 | `pct_period` | daily | old-rule savings return (see below) |
| `USDBRL` | 1 | `brl_per_usd` | daily | SGS's BRL/USD selling rate |
| `EURBRL` | 21619 | `brl_per_eur` | daily | SGS's BRL/EUR selling rate |
| `PIB` | 4380 | `brl_million` | monthly | monthly GDP, R$ millions, current prices |

An IPCA label or code is refused with a pointer to `inflation`; anything else
unknown raises `22023` listing the nine. Default window: 12 months for the daily
series, 120 for the monthly ones.

### Three series that are easy to misread

- **`SELIC_META` is published ahead.** BACEN dates the target per calendar day
  and carries it forward to the next Copom meeting (on 2026-09-25 the series
  already ran to 2026-11-04). The default window ends today; an explicit `p_to`
  after today can return those forward-dated rows.
- **`CDI` and `SELIC_DIARIA` are per business day.** `0.050788` is % per day,
  not per year. Compounding it to an annual rate is your calculation — do it in
  the notebook and say so.
- **`POUPANCA` is the old-rule return, per anniversary day.** SGS 25 is BACEN's
  _Depósitos de poupança até 03.05.2012 – Rentabilidade no período_: one value
  per anniversary day, each the return over the month that starts on that date.
  It is not a calendar-month figure and not the return of deposits made after
  2012-05-03.

### PTAX

One row per currency and business day: `buy_rate` (compra) and `sell_rate`
(venda), **BRL per one unit** of the currency — JPY and ARS included. The
currencies are the ones Silo ingests (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, ARS); an unknown code
raises `22023` listing the ones held.

BACEN prints up to five bulletins a day (opening, three intermediate, closing).
Silo keeps the last one it received, which for a completed day is the closing
**PTAX** — measured on 2026-09-22/23, the stored USD sell rate equals SGS 1 and
BACEN's _Fechamento_ bulletin to four decimals. The bulletin type is not stored.
A holiday has no row; nothing is filled.

### Freshness

[`coverage()`](/api-docs/coverage) has a `macro_series` row (the newest elapsed
observation of any of the nine — their cadences differ, so read a monthly
series' own last row before calling it late) and a `ptax` row. Each `landed_at`
is its own ingest: an SGS run never reports as PTAX freshness, or the reverse.

### Limits

Both refuse above one 1,000-row page (`22023`); narrow `p_from` / `p_to`. A daily
series fits about three years per call.
